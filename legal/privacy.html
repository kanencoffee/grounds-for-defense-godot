Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 5, 2026 · Last Updated: May 5, 2026

Grounds for Defense is a free browser game published by Kanen Coffee, LLC. ("we", "us", "our"). This Privacy Policy explains what information is and isn't collected when you play the game at this website.

1. The short version

We don't collect personally identifiable information from you. The game runs entirely in your browser. We don't have user accounts, login, or analytics on your gameplay.

2. What we don't collect

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or other personal contact details
  • Your location (we don't ask for or use geolocation)
  • Your gameplay statistics, scores, or progress (the game doesn't transmit any data anywhere)
  • Tracking cookies, advertising identifiers, or fingerprinting data
  • Any third-party analytics (no Google Analytics, no Meta Pixel, no other trackers)

3. Browser storage

The game uses your browser's standard cache to store the game files (HTML, JavaScript, images, audio) so subsequent visits load faster. This is the same caching every website uses. You can clear it from your browser settings at any time. The game does not currently use cookies or localStorage to store user data.

4. Third parties

The game is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service operated by GitHub, Inc. When you visit the site, GitHub may collect standard server logs (IP address, browser type, request timestamps) for the purposes of operating their service and preventing abuse. We do not have access to these logs. GitHub's privacy practices are described at GitHub's Privacy Statement.

The game also loads the Phaser game framework from jsDelivr, a public content-delivery network. jsDelivr's privacy policy is at jsdelivr.com/terms/privacy-policy.

5. Children's privacy

The game is suitable for all ages. Because we do not collect any personal information from any user, we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected information from a child, please contact us at service@kanencoffee.com and we will take steps to investigate and remove it.

6. California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, the CCPA grants you certain rights regarding your personal information. Because we do not collect, sell, or share any personal information, there is nothing for us to disclose, delete, or opt-out of. You may still exercise your rights by contacting us at service@kanencoffee.com.

7. European Union (GDPR)

If you are in the European Union, the GDPR grants you rights including access, rectification, erasure, and objection. Because we do not collect personal data, these rights are not implicated by playing this game. Standard server logs maintained by GitHub Pages (see above) are governed by GitHub's privacy policy.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last Updated" date at the top reflects the most recent change. Continued use of the game after a change constitutes acceptance.
